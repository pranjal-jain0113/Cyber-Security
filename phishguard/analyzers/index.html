Typosquatting Trickery

Attackers register lookalike domains swapping characters (e.g., using zeros for 'o' or hyphens) to spoof high-trust corporate brands.

Misleading SSL Illusion

Having an SSL certificate (HTTPS) only means encrypted transit—it does not prove the site owner is legitimate or trustworthy.

Zero-Trust Verification

Always navigate directly to official bookmarks or applications rather than clicking links embedded inside unexpected emails or SMS.
